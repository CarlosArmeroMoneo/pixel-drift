# Privacy

Pixel Drift is an offline Android app.

- No network permission and no telemetry, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK.
- No account, cloud synchronization, location, contacts, microphone, camera, or sensor access.
- Artwork is selected through Android's system document picker. The app validates it and stores a
  private local copy so the wallpaper remains available if the original moves.
- App backup and device-to-device data extraction are disabled.
- Uninstalling Pixel Drift deletes its private settings and artwork copy. It does not delete the
  original document selected through Android.

Android and the device manufacturer may independently collect operating-system diagnostics under
their own policies. Pixel Drift does not transmit data to its developer.
